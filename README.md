# ShadowFox Data Analyst – Intermediate Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship – Intermediate Level.

The project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand customer behavior, product performance, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, and sales trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify repeat customers
- Analyze customer and product revenue
- Compare sales and profit across categories
- Analyze regional and segment performance
- Identify sales trends over time
- Create a business dashboard
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a Superstore sales dataset containing information related to:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Customer Segments
- Sales
- Profit
- Order Dates

The original dataset was preserved separately, while a cleaned copy was used for analysis.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was checked and prepared before analysis.

The following checks were performed:

- Duplicate records were checked
- Blank values were checked
- Sales values were checked for negative values
- Quantity values were checked for negative values
- Profit values were reviewed

Negative profit values were retained because they represent loss-making transactions and are important for profitability analysis.

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Analysis

Customer purchasing behavior was analyzed using Customer ID and Order ID.

The analysis identified **781 repeat customers**.

A Top 10 customer analysis was also performed based on sales.

### 2. Product Analysis

Products were analyzed based on sales and profit.

The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the highest-selling product among the Top 10 products.

The analysis also showed that some products generated negative or zero profit despite having sales.

### 3. Category Analysis

Sales and profit were compared across product categories.

- Technology had the highest sales and profit.
- Office Supplies had the lowest sales.
- Furniture had the lowest profit.

### 4. Regional Analysis

Sales and profit were analyzed across different regions.

- West had the highest sales and profit.
- South had the lowest sales.
- Central had the lowest profit.

### 5. Segment Analysis

Customer segments were compared based on sales and profit.

- Consumer had the highest sales and profit.
- Home Office had the lowest sales and profit.

### 6. Trend Analysis

Sales and profit were analyzed over time.

The analysis showed that **2017 had the highest annual sales**, while **2015 had the lowest annual sales**.

## 📈 Dashboard

An Excel dashboard was created to provide a summarized view of business performance.

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Repeat Customers
- Top 10 Customers
- Top 10 Products
- Category Performance
- Regional Performance
- Sales and Profit Trend

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology is a strong contributor to both sales and profit.
- Some high-selling products are not necessarily profitable.
- West is the strongest performing region based on sales and profit.
- Consumer is the strongest customer segment.
- 781 repeat customers were identified.
- Annual sales were highest in 2017 and lowest in 2015.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Continue supporting strong-performing categories such as Technology.
- Review pricing, discounts, and costs for low-profit categories such as Furniture.
- Investigate products generating negative or zero profit.
- Improve sales performance in the South region.
- Develop targeted strategies for the Home Office segment.
- Focus on customer retention and repeat purchases.

## 📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used for practical data analysis and business decision-making.

The analysis combines data cleaning, customer analysis, product profitability, category and regional analysis, trend analysis, visualization, and business recommendations to provide a structured view of Superstore performance.
